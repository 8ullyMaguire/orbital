How this works

{{ layout.site_name }} is a directory of people and the things they make. One page per person, one leaderboard per craft. That is the whole idea.

Verticals

A vertical is a themed list — developers, writers, musicians, indie web. Every member picks one primary vertical and up to two secondaries. Secondaries rank at 0.7×, so listing in every category is not a strategy: focus wins.

How the leaderboards are measured

Top is cumulative distinct visitors, all time. A monument, no decay.

Trending is distinct visitors over the last seven days, normalised against the busiest member of that vertical, with a small logarithmic boost for new accounts and a trust multiplier (0.5× at trust level 0, up to 1.1× at 3). If nobody in a vertical has traffic this week, the tab is hidden rather than filled with whoever joined last.

New is chronological. Join early and you are visible immediately.

Curated is hand-picked and manually ordered. It is the editorial layer, clearly separate from the measured one.

What counts as a visitor

Each outbound click records the vertical you were looking at, the slot you clicked, and a salted hash of a first-party cookie. No IP addresses, no user agents, no third-party scripts, no cross-site tracking. Distinct sessions are what the leaderboards count, so a hundred clicks from one browser count once. Raw click events are deleted after 180 days.

Trust levels

New accounts are trust level 0 and are dampened in Trending. Level 1 is earned automatically after a few days with real links and real visitors. Levels 2 and 3 are granted by the curator. Adult listings require level 1.

The other half of the loop

Profiles link out with a source-tagged URL, and they link back to their Bluesky account when it is verified. The sidebar on a profile suggests people who share your tags. The footer walks the webring: previous and next within the vertical, previous and next across everybody.

What it does not do

No engagement feed, no infinite scroll, no notification emails, no ad slots, no selling of the click data. A profile is a page, and a leaderboard is a measurement.
